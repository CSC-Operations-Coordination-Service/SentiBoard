DEVOCS-219: align both apps with the ESA brand — NotesEsa type and SentiBoard cyan

Typography, colour and page furniture brought into line with the SentiBoard
installation, applied to the Vite mock-ups and the Next.js frontend together so
the two cannot drift.

TYPE — NotesEsa replaces Inter and Space Grotesk. Four faces (regular, italic,
bold, bold-italic) are committed in both apps: frontend/app/fonts/ loaded through
next/font/local, and design/react-mockups/public/ for the mock-ups. Body text now
falls back to the system stack rather than shipping a second family, so --font-sans
starts at Helvetica Neue and --font-display is NotesEsa.

COLOUR — the accent moves to the logo cyan. --accent becomes #12b1bf (was the
telemetry blue #2e7df6) across the mock-up tokens, with the hairline, pill and
grid tints re-derived from it. The Next app gains --primary-dark #1B4D3E and
--primary-light #2DB882, which now drive primary buttons and the page-description
panel instead of the blue accent.

PAGEHEADER — one component per app (breadcrumbs, title, collapsible description,
optional background image), plus page-header.css in the mock-ups, replacing the
per-page header markup the proposal pages each carried.

INDEX — a new /examples/index1 landing in both apps, carrying the module cards and,
in the Next copy, an ExpandableNewsBar. The mock-ups' Nav points its Index link and
brand mark at it. The About entry is removed from both navs and
frontend/app/v1/about/page.tsx is deleted with it.

ICON FONTS — Flaticon and Simple-Line-Icons (woff/woff2) added under
frontend/public/assets so the ported /v1 pages render their glyphs offline.

Four implementation write-ups ride along: design/react-mockups/DESIGN-UPDATES.md
and, under frontend/, CHANGELOG-TYPOGRAPHY.md, TYPOGRAPHY-UPDATE.md and
DEVOCS-219-IMPLEMENTATION.md.
